Whale shark tour

Snorkel around the biggest and gentlest fish in the ocean: The whale shark! Did you know? Whale sharks filter feed on plankton just like whales do, that is why they are called whale sharks. This snorkelling trip is perfect for anyone who wants to learn more about our ocean and its creatures. Kids are more than welcome, they will guaranteedly enjoy themselves. Not a swimmer? Not a problem! We will take care of your safety while you are in the water.

  • Maximum group size: 8 person.
  • Age limits: 5-60*
  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Location: Toya port, Yomitan
  • Schedule:
    • 1st tour: 8.00
    • 2nd tour: 10.00
    • 3rd tour: 12.30

* If you are older, please contact us, as if you are in good overall health we can still take you. During the trip we will go on a boat so if you are prone to motion or seasickness, please bring sea sickness medication with you. Pictures are included in the package. Pick-up is available from Uruma, Okinawa City, Kadena and Yomitan for 5000 yen. If you are staying somewhere else, we cannot offer pick up.

What to bring?

  • Sunscreen
  • Swimsuit
  • Hat
  • Plenty of water
  • Sunglasses
  • Towel
  • Dry clothes for after the snorkelling tour

We provide you with the gear for snorkelling: life jacket, snorkel, mask, fins, and wetsuit are included in the package, weather dependently.
