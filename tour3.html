Japanese cooking class

Prepare your own Japanese meal with a Japanese chef in an izakaya (traditional Japanese eatery) with an English interpreter! Enjoy learning the secrets of Japanese tastes and make your own meal which you can eat afterwards. This tour is perfect for foodies and cuisine enthusiasts. If you like dedicating time to cooking, learning new recipes and engaging in authentic activities then this cooking class is your best choice!

  • Maximum group size: 4 people
  • Start time:10.00
  • Duration: 2.5 hours
  • Location: Ryukyu Cusine Tam, Onna, close to Hotel Renaissance

Everyday except for Tuesday! No pick up available.
