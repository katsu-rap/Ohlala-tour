Calligraphy class

Do you have an artistic vein? Are you interested in the art of Japanese writing? Simply obsessed with Japan? This course is the perfect pick for you then! Write any short sentences, words, names whatever you choose with a Japanese sensei! You will learn how to shape the kanjis and kanas correctly using a traditional Japanese brush on rice paper. It seems hard, but your teachers will guide you through it and you can take home the result! English and Japanese speaker teachers available. Everyone can participate who can already write (in any language). Tea is served during the class.

  • Maximum group size: 4 people*
  • Start time: Custom
  • Duration: 1-1.5 hour
  • Location: Cafés, beaches, hotels, it will depend on the day

*Bigger group sizes might be OK, please contact us to confirm. Pick-up from Uruma, Okinawa City, Kadena, Yomitan is available for 5000 yen.

What you made is a suvenir for you, and you can make it a scroll in a gift box which would be 1000 yen plus. Japanese and English speaker teachers available. Japanese tea of your choice is included. Tea list: Roiboos, Japanese barley, Japanese green tea, Ceylon and Earl gray.
